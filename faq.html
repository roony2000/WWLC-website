Registering

How do I register?
  1. Create your account on the Register page with your name, email address and a password.
  2. Enter the six-digit code we send to your email to verify your account.
  3. Sign in and start your placement test.

Go to the Register page

I did not receive my verification code. What should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first. The code expires after a short time, so if it has expired, use "Resend code" on the Register page to get a new one.
