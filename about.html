About Wordsworth Language Centre

From fast-track IELTS bootcamps to lively junior clubs, our supportive teachers help you speak with clarity, fluency, and heart.

Who we are

Wordsworth Language Centre is an English language centre in Kuala Lumpur. We help adults and young learners master English with immersive courses, expert teachers and a supportive community.

Our courses cover every stage of life: general, IELTS and business English for adults; Cambridge exam preparation, public speaking and study skills for teens; and phonics and confidence clubs for young learners.

Our placement test helps us recommend the best class pathway for your goals. Outside the classroom, learners join community trips and events, from waterfall and hill walks to a treasure hunt.
